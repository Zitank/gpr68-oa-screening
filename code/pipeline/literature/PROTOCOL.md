# Literature protocol (L0)

Screening codes are written **before** any screening. The librarian maintains this file.

## Sources

Search OpenAlex, Crossref, and arXiv (abs HTML and, when it answers, `export.arxiv.org`). Semantic Scholar is attempted without a key. Google Scholar remains a human pass. **Live HTTP results:** `literature/capability_report.md` (`just lit-capability`) — do not copy stale network folklore into records. Tools degrade to recorded `source unavailable`, never to memory.

## Seed expansion

The five seed reading notes are not shipped in this repository. Resolve them into `literature/corpus/seed_records.jsonl` when the notes are available. The notes are `@huang2015allosteric`, `@matsingos2023elucidating` (`is_preprint: true`), `@krivak2018p2rank`, `@chen2019hidden` and `@hyde2026joint`. They are vault reading notes, not corpus citekeys, until this resolution is done. Unresolved identifiers stay flagged. Cluster Boolean queries live in `literature/queries/` (copy `TEMPLATE.yaml` to `<cluster>.yaml`). `just lit-search <cluster>` appends new rows to `records.jsonl` and a `runs:` entry (date_utc, source, n_returned, n_new).

## Clusters

- **gpr68-pharm** — GPR68 pharmacology: modulators, assays, potency, orthosteric versus allosteric action. Not cartilage phenotypes and not docking-method papers.
- **gpcr-proton** — GPCR structure and proton sensing: experimental GPR68 structures, proton-sensing residues, activation at acidic pH. Not ligand-SAR tables except where they locate a site.
- **docking-decoy** — docking benchmarking and decoy design: enrichment metrics, property-matched decoys, hidden bias in decoy sets, Vina-family scoring. Not a GPR68 biology review.
- **oa-cartilage** — OA cartilage biology: acidosis, GPR68 in cartilage, matrix turnover (MMP13, ADAMTS5, COL2A1, ACAN). Not docking protocols.

A paper may sit in more than one cluster only when the screening note says which question each cluster is using it for. Do not file a methods paper under oa-cartilage because the introduction mentions osteoarthritis.

## Snowballing (L2)

Two rounds, backward (references) and forward (cited-by), from every T1/T2 candidate. Use **two graphs** for forward chasing (OpenAlex + a second graph the human can reach). Stop a cluster when a round adds **<5%** new would-be T2+ items. Query ids: `snowball:fwd:<id>`, `snowball:bwd:<id>`.

## Dedupe

Order: `doi` → `arxiv_id` → `norm(title)+first-author-family+year`. Merge preprint+published into one record, keep both ids, `is_preprint=false`, union `found_in`, `queries`, `provenance`. Prefer published venue. `just lit-dedupe`.

## Screening (apply at L3; codes frozen now)

Include: **I1** relevant to a cluster question; **I2** primary technical content or an authoritative review; **I3** enough methodological detail to judge. Exclude: **E1** duplicate; **E2** off-topic; **E3** insufficient information (press, marketing); **E4** retracted; **E5** not accessible (keep `unavailable`, ask the human). Do **not** auto-exclude non-English items from relevant groups; flag for the human.

## Tiering

**T1** core (constrains design or novelty, or is a constant source). **T2** important (methods or results needed to place the work). **T3** context. **T4** candidate-only. Rationale is required (`literature/corpus/tiers.yaml` at L3; never leave `tier` set without it).

## Retraction and status

`retracted` is bool or null from source metadata. `is_preprint` vs published is stored on the record; cards must show both.

## Recency

No fixed cutoff. Foundational works stay regardless of age. Prefer the last few years for OA cartilage biology and for experimental GPR68 structures. No recency cutoff for foundational pharmacology or for docking-benchmark papers.

## Access

IEEE Xplore and other paywalled full text (`ieee-no-ai` / `paywalled-no-ai`) are **never** requested, parsed, stored in `sources/`, or reasoned over. Metadata (Crossref/OpenAlex) is fine. OA text is fetched only by the optional vault tool `tools/fetch_arxiv.py` into the vault `sources/<key>.md`, never into this repo. Abstracts and ≤25-word snippets only. `just lit-fetch <arxiv_id> <citekey>` (see `KIT.md` for the path).

## What the human searches by hand

1. Google Scholar forward citations of Huang et al. 2015 (`10.1038/nature15699`) and of the GPR68 proton-sensing preprint (`10.1101/2023.12.08.570878`). OpenAlex cited-by is only one graph.
2. Paywalled full text via the ZJU library, into Zotero only. Never commit PDFs. The 8Z68 primary paper, once it leaves "To be published", is checked on this path.
3. Groups to watch: Roth / Shoichet (GPR68 ligand discovery), Fornili (proton-sensing mechanism), Hoksza (P2Rank), and whoever publishes the peer-reviewed 8Z68 structure.
4. Chinese-language OA and cartilage literature (CNKI) is a human pass. Access policy is unverified. Do not drop Chinese records at screening.
5. Venues: Journal of Medicinal Chemistry and the Nature family for GPR68 ligands; Journal of Cheminformatics for pockets and decoys; Osteoarthritis and Cartilage, including OARSI abstracts that are not full papers.
6. Negative-result queries: docking does not enrich; decoy bias; the allosteric site is not the orthosteric pocket; GPR68 modulation does not protect cartilage; PAM versus orthosteric ligand.
7. Code and data named by the seeds: PDB 8Z68, ChEMBL CHEMBL3713916, PubChem AID 2060853, P2Rank, GNINA (CUDA-only; not runnable on this machine), smina. The MCE 5K SDF is a screening input, not a paper supplement. If it is missing, label the substitute; do not swap it silently.

## Integrity

Every record needs provenance (source, url, retrieved_utc). `just lit-corpus-check` / `--online`. Canary: `tools/check_corpus.py --planted-fake-test`. After `corpus-v1`, `just lit-update` re-runs queries since the last run date into an inbox; no automatic promotion.
